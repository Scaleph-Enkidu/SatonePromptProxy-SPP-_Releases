# Meta performance

[Documentation](../README.md#systems) · AIChat 1.18.30 + SPP 5.10.10

This scene grew out of a concrete limitation: Satone cannot reliably read the current window view. Persistently asking why she will not look outside can turn ordinary evasive conversation into a special performance. The author's reaction was that even a text-only scene felt scarier than expected.

## Controls and reset

The enable switch and reset button are under the Meta horror performance settings. Reset takes effect immediately in the same game session; each new game launch also starts at stage 1. Progress belongs to the game session, not a memory profile's long-term affection. Switching service, model, persona or profile does not reset it.

<details>
<summary>Triggers and endings (spoilers)</summary>

Repeated questions about the current view or whether she can really see outside are assessed for eligibility, then advance through four ordered stages. Not every mention of a window qualifies, and it is not a simple keyword counter. Timing and cooldowns matter; scattered questions need not accumulate indefinitely.

She first avoids the topic, followed by disconnection and visual glitches. The current model participates in the dialogue, so wording varies.

After the first complete performance in a session, the glitch/disconnection remains. If reset in that same session and completed again, the ending **exits the game normally**, after the required turn records and cleanup conditions. It does not forcibly kill the process.

Original chapters remain accessible during the first glitch state. AI input and some original random behaviors follow disconnection protection. Switching model or affection cannot unlock it; reset the performance or restart the game. The original game's own disconnection state still follows its story.

Session performance records are cleared on the next game launch. Ordinary chat remains. This does not delete profiles, long-term memories or all history.

</details>

This scene does not provide screen vision, complete appearance reading or control of game items. Those need a catalog and further integration. The [F10 tool](../tools/STATE_CATALOG.md) only collects/describes currently active appearances.
